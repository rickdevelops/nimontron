Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy covers orders for Nimontron invitation websites. It is part of our Terms of Service.

Cancelling before we start

If you cancel before we have started work on your invitation, we refund everything you paid.

Cancelling after we start

Once work has started, the advance is not refundable, because the design and build time has already been spent. If you paid in full and cancel after work has started, we keep the 50% advance and refund the other half. We will not charge you a balance for work we did not do.

If we cannot deliver

If we are unable to deliver your invitation, we refund everything you paid.

Duplicate or wrong payments

If you were charged twice or paid the wrong amount, tell us the payment ID and we refund the difference.

Failed payments

If a payment fails but money left your account, your bank normally returns it within a few working days. If it doesn't, send us the payment ID and we will help you trace it.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. They usually reach your account within 5 to 7 working days after we approve them.

How to ask

Message us with your names and payment ID.
