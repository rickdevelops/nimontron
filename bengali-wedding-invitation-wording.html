Three lines you see on almost every Bengali wedding card

  • শ্রী শ্রী প্রজাপতয়ে নমঃ"Salutations to Prajapati." Hindu Bengali wedding cards traditionally open with this line at the very top. Other families open with a line from their own tradition, such as বিসমিল্লাহির রাহমানির রাহিম.
  • সবিনয় নিবেদন"With humble submission." The formal opening of the invitation itself, like "Dear Sir or Madam" on a letter.
  • পত্র দ্বারা নিমন্ত্রণের ত্রুটি মার্জনীয়"Please forgive us for inviting you by letter." By custom an invitation is given in person, so the card apologises for arriving on its own. The same line works on a link sent by WhatsApp.

Traditional wedding invitation (from the bride's parents)

The classic card, written in the formal sadhu style. The groom's family uses the same shape with the names the other way round.

বাংলা

শ্রী শ্রী প্রজাপতয়ে নমঃ

সবিনয় নিবেদন,
আগামী [বাংলা তারিখ] (ইং [ইংরেজি তারিখ]), [বার], আমাদের কন্যা কল্যাণীয়া [কনের নাম]-এর সহিত [ঠিকানা] নিবাসী শ্রী [বরের বাবার নাম] মহাশয়ের পুত্র কল্যাণীয় শ্রীমান [বরের নাম]-এর শুভ বিবাহ অনুষ্ঠিত হইবে।

এই শুভ অনুষ্ঠানে আপনি সবান্ধবে উপস্থিত থাকিয়া নবদম্পতিকে আশীর্বাদ করিলে বাধিত হইব।

বিনীত
[কনের বাবা ও মায়ের নাম]

বিবাহ বাসর: [ভেন্যুর নাম ও ঠিকানা]
পত্র দ্বারা নিমন্ত্রণের ত্রুটি মার্জনীয়।

English

With the blessings of our elders, we request the pleasure of your company at the wedding of our daughter

[Bride's name]
with
[Groom's name]
son of Mr and Mrs [Groom's parents' names]

on [day, date] at [time]
at [venue name and address].

Your presence and blessings will make the day complete.

With warm regards,
[Bride's parents' names]

English

To celebrate the wedding of our son [Groom's name] with [Bride's name], we warmly invite you and your family to the Bou Bhat and reception

Please join us for the Annaprashan (first rice ceremony) of our [son / daughter]

[Parents' names]

What every invitation should include

  • Names of the bride and groom, and usually both sets of parents.
  • The date in both calendars (for example a date in Magh or Falgun beside the English date) and the day of the week. Take the Bengali date and the lagna from your family's panjika or purohit.
  • The time, and the lagna if your family prints it.
  • The venue with a landmark. "Near Karunamoyee bus stand" saves a dozen phone calls.
  • Every event the guest is invited to: Gaye Holud, Biye, Bou Bhat. The bride's side and the groom's side often send different lists.
  • Who is inviting (নিবেদক) and a way to reply.

Customs differ from family to family and region to region. Treat these samples as a starting point and check the final wording with the elders at home.
