Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This page explains what information Nimontron collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you contact us (the contact form or Instagram): your message and the details you choose to share, such as names, event dates, venues and photos. The contact form reaches our inbox through a form service called Web3Forms.

When you pay: payments are handled by Razorpay. Razorpay collects your payment details and contact details directly. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive a payment confirmation and payment ID so we can match it to your order.

On your invitation site: if your guests send an RSVP, the name, reply, head count and note they enter are sent to you by email. Only you and we can see them.

Visitors to nimontron.shop: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.

How we use it

To reply to you, make and publish your invitation, take payment, and run the service. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

Only the services we rely on to run Nimontron: Razorpay (payments), Cloudflare (hosting and analytics), GitHub (website hosting) and Web3Forms (contact form).

How long we keep it

RSVP data is deleted 90 days after your event. Messages and order details are kept only as long as we need them to run the service and answer questions about your order.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or to take your invitation site down, at any time. Message us using the details below.
