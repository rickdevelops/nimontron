How the free invitation link works

1. Fill in the details

Pick the occasion, type the names, date, time and venue, and choose Bangla or English. A suggested invitation message is filled in for you and you can change every word.

2. Get your link

One tap makes your invitation link. There is no signup, no app and no payment. Your details are packed into the link itself and are not saved on our servers.

3. Share on WhatsApp

Send the link to family and friends. Guests see the invitation, open the venue in Google Maps and add the date to their calendar.

Questions

Is the invitation maker really free?

Yes. Making and sharing an invitation link is free, with no signup. The invitation shows a small "Made with Nimontron" line. Paid invitation websites add RSVP, a venue map with landmarks, several events, photos and both languages.

Can I write the invitation in Bengali?

Yes. Choose Bengali and type the names, venue and message in Bangla. Dates, times and buttons then show in Bengali too.

Where are my details stored?

Nowhere. The names, date and venue are packed into the link itself, so only people who have the link can see them.

Can guests RSVP on the free invitation?

No. The free link shows the date, time, venue, a Google Maps button and an add-to-calendar button. One-tap RSVP with head counts is part of the paid invitation websites, from ₹699.
