Questions

What families usually ask.

Can elders use it?

Yes. It opens from WhatsApp like any link, the text is large, and Bengali is one tap away. No app, no login.

Can we still print cards?

Yes. Classic and Royal include a QR code you can put on your printed card so guests open the site from it.

How long does it stay live?

One year from the first event. We can archive it as a keepsake after that.

Can we have our own domain name?

Yes. Tell us the name you want and we'll check what's available and quote the price. It may be .in or .online rather than .com.

What do you need from us?

Names, event dates and times, venues, and the family names to print. Photos are optional.

Who sees the RSVP replies?

Only you. Replies go to your email. We delete RSVP data 90 days after the event.

Can you do other ceremonies?

Yes: Annaprashan, Griha Probesh, birthdays and anniversaries. Message us with the date.

Getting married between 20 November and 12 December?

Invites usually go out three weeks before. Send us your names today and see your preview tonight.
